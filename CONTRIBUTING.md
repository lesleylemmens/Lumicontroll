# Contributing to LumiControLL

Contributions are welcome when they are intended for the original LumiControLL project.

Please do not publish modified versions, forks, or derivative releases as separate products or under another name. Fixes, translations, documentation updates, and improvements should be submitted back to this project for review.

By submitting a contribution to LumiControLL, you grant Lesley Lemmens permission to use, modify, publish, and distribute that contribution as part of LumiControLL.

## Development Notes

- Keep generated files out of commits: `build/`, `dist/`, `installer_output/`, `__pycache__/`.
- Keep personal runtime files out of commits: `settings.config`, `sound_settings.json`, `adm.config`, `scenes.config`, and `shows/`.
- Installer defaults belong in `installer_defaults/`.
- Third-party license notices belong in `third_party_licenses/`.
